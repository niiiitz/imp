Why IMP?

Hybrid Audio & Video Queues

Mix local FLAC/MP3/MP4 files, Steam Soundtracks, Apple Music, SoundCloud, Internet Archive films & concerts, and optical Audio CDs / DVDs in one playlist.

12 Real-Time Visualizers

High-DPI reactive visualizers paired with time-synced lyrics (LRCLIB), timed SoundCloud comments, VHS track chyrons, and 10-band EQ.

Gamepad, VR & Desktop Ready

XMB-inspired controller navigation for Steam Deck, living-room TVs, and SteamVR — switching seamlessly to mouse & keyboard on desktop.

Zero-Bloat Background Daemon

Close the window and keep listening from the Windows System Tray, macOS Menu Bar, or SteamOS background service with native media-key controls.
