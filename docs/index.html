Why IMP?

Unified Library & Hybrid Queues

Mix local FLAC/MP3/MP4 folders, Steam Soundtracks, Apple Music, SoundCloud, Internet Archive films & concerts, and CDs/DVDs across fixed square-tile shelves and See All › pop-outs.

12 Gaming-Friendly Visualizers

Tuned 30 FPS default (20 / 30 / 40 / Uncapped) for dual-monitor & Deck gaming, paired with synced lyrics (LRCLIB), timed comments, and a 10-band EQ.

3-Column XMB & UI Scaling

Focus-isolated controller navigation across Display, Audio & Sources, and System & Network, with automatic UI scaling from 800p handhelds to 4K/ultrawide TVs.

Granular Privacy & Native Tray

Independently toggle online Lyrics, Artwork, Factoids, Album/DVD Track Data, and SoundCloud Comments while keeping native tray, Menu Bar, MPRIS2, and OpenVR playback running.
